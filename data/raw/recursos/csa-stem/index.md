Space resources for STEM and youth organizations
================================================

![Two boys and two girls lay their hands on a giant Earth globe.](/images/jeunes-educateurs/ressources-organisations-specialisees-en-stim-organismes-jeunesse-id-19484-ban.jpg)

Are you an educator in a:

* science centre or museum?
* youth or STEM outreach organization?
* space or summer camp?
* library?
* after-school club?
* community group?
* any other organization catering to youth?

We believe that helping Canadian youth get excited about space and STEM is a shared responsibility. That's why our initiatives are tailored to the needs of teachers in the classroom, but also of **educators in more informal learning settings** and parents, whether or not they homeschool their kids.

So if you are looking for free, bilingual tools, activities and resources to build programming about space for the youth you work with, you've come to the right place!
